## External video tool prompt

Create an eight-second seamless product-film loop for Cadentra, 3840 x 2160, 24 fps, silent. A physically plausible continuous ribbon of translucent mint glass with a precision brushed graphite-metal edge rests in a near-black studio. Premium product cinematography, crisp refractions, controlled mint rim lighting, rich blacks and a subtly reflective floor. Match the supplied reference image in material, lighting and composition. The sculpture occupies the lower and right portions; preserve clean dark negative space in the upper-left for website text. Use an extremely slow, subtle rotation with matching first and last frames. No cuts, camera shake, particles, neon effects, people, logos, text, numbers, dashboards or interface elements. Maintain stable geometry. Deliver a high-quality master and matching first-frame poster. Also provide a separately composed 9:16 mobile version with the same materials and lighting.

## After the clip is supplied
